About Vehicle Sathi

Vehicle Sathi is being built to make vehicle maintenance and assistance easier for vehicle owners by connecting them with service providers through a unified digital platform.

Our Vision

Vehicle Sathi aims to build a connected vehicle-service ecosystem where vehicle owners can conveniently discover and access vehicle services, roadside assistance, and vehicle-related products through one platform. Today, Vehicle Sathi operates across 5 cities in West Bengal (Durgapur, Bardhaman, Asansol, Berhampur, and Raniganj), with a vision to expand city by city.

Our Mission

To make vehicle service simpler, more accessible, and more organized for vehicle owners and local service providers.
